\# Research Specification — AI News and Stock Market Risk



\## 1. Reference Paper



Salisu, A. A., \& Badmus, S. (2026).



\*Can AI News Improve the Out-of-Sample Forecasts of Risk Premia and Volatility in the U.S. Stock Market?\*



Journal: Quality \& Quantity.



DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11135-026-02816-9



\## 2. Research Period



\- Start date: January 2012

\- End date: December 2024

\- Frequency: Monthly

\- Market: United States



\## 3. Variables



\### Dependent variables

\- Stock market risk premium

\- Stock return volatility



\### Main explanatory variables

\- AI News indicator

\- AI Web Search indicator



Both indicators are based on Google Trends data.



\### Control variables

\- US inflation

\- CBOE VIX (market volatility index)



\### Additional variable

\- AI ETF price or return measure.



\## 4. Stock Classification



Two stock categories:



\- AI-related stocks

\- Conventional stocks



The AI stock classification must be verified against the exact list used in the original paper.



\## 5. Risk Premium Construction



Risk premium = Nominal stock return − Risk-free interest rate.



The exact stock return calculation, risk-free rate series and maturity must be verified before implementation.



\## 6. Econometric Methodology



The paper uses the Westerlund–Narayan approach and FQGLS estimation.



The predictive specifications involve:

\- Lagged dependent variables

\- Lagged AI indicators

\- First differences of AI indicators

\- Macroeconomic controls



Exact equations and transformations must be checked against the published article.



\## 7. Forecasting



Forecast evaluation includes:



\- Historical average benchmark

\- Random walk benchmark

\- Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)

\- Clark–West forecast comparison test



Forecast horizons to verify:

\- 3 months

\- 6 months

\- 12 months



\## 8. Items Requiring Verification



Before collecting data, verify:



1\. Exact stock return construction.

2\. Exact volatility construction.

3\. Treasury bill series and maturity.

4\. Inflation series and transformation.

5\. Exact list of AI-related stocks.

6\. AI ETF ticker and price transformation.

7\. Estimation and forecasting sample split.

8\. Exact FQGLS equations and implementation.

9\. Exact forecasting horizons and benchmark specifications.



\## 9. Data Sources to Investigate



\- Google Trends: AI News and AI Web Search.

\- FRED: US macroeconomic and financial indicators.

\- Stock Analysis PRO: stock-level data, as used in the paper.

\- ETF Database: AI-related ETF classification.



Any replacement data source must be documented.



\## 10. Research Transparency



The project must distinguish between:



\- Direct replication of the original paper.

\- Data-source substitutions.

\- Methodological adaptations.

\- Additional robustness checks and research extensions.



Never modify data or model specifications simply to obtain results similar to the published paper.
